How do the dice work?
d20 · Destiny
Your name and trade lead. The d20 nudges Jev: a natural 20 invites a legendary calling; a natural 1 invites a little chaos. A clear class match can win on any roll.
d6 · Quirk
Once the class is chosen, the d6 picks one of six original quirks for it. A little personality, with every roll.

The d20 is a prompt hint, with no fixed numerical bonus. The d6 is a local lookup. One quick Jev decision ties it together.

Every class has a story.
Yours probably includes snacks.
